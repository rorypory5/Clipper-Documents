Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Clip Helper is a personal tool used only by its owner, Rory Keyser.

What the app accesses

When the owner logs in with TikTok, the app receives an access token that lets it upload videos to the owner's own account. It does not read other users' data.

How data is stored

The access token is stored only on the owner's own computer. No data is collected from other people, and nothing is sold, shared or sent to any third party.

Videos

Videos are processed on the owner's computer and sent only to the owner's TikTok account.

Deleting data

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok's settings and delete the stored token file.
